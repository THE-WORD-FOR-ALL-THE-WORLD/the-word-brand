# Superseded Revival To My City wordmark

The heavy DM Sans lockup with the Flame tick and the endorsement line, approved from
Brand Guide v5.4 until v6.1. Replaced by the serif mark whose O-bracket field carries
the city name, published at `assets/logos/rtmc/`.

Kept because `archive/` holds every retired form, so an audit citing an older guide can
still be checked against what that guide actually showed. Not a published asset: it does
not appear in `/ai/assets.json`, and no live page may link to it.
