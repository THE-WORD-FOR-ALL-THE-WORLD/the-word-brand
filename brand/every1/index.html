What kind of brand this is

A movement brand.

EVERY1 lives on phones and in other people's feeds. It carries the loosest rules of the three, the strongest single glyph, and assets designed to be given away. The full-bleed Midnight, the footage, and the pace are the right instincts; the participation layer below is what makes it a movement.

Place in the process

Why this door exists.

BURN is the second movement. A cleaned heart catches fire, and fire starts fire. This door is not an event, it is a lifestyle: ordinary believers sharing Jesus where they already live, work, and study. Membership is simple, and it is measured by whether you have shared Jesus with someone recently.

The participation layer

Assets designed to be given away.

A movement's identity lives on its people's own feeds, or it is not a movement. These assets exist to leave official hands.

  • The 1 is the glyph. The mark leads with the numeral, and the numeral is published on its own: an app icon, a profile badge, a sticker, a shape simple enough to survive at sixty pixels with no endorsement line in frame.
  • Share cards ride along. Every official EVERY1 surface ships with a version a member can post themselves: square and vertical, footage behind the scrim, one line of Parchment type.
  • The loosest rules in the house. Official surfaces follow this guide. What a member does with the badge on their own feed is not audited; it is the movement working.
  • The icon comes first. The app and the activation platform follow the YouVersion model, so the E1 icon carries the whole identity on its own. It was drawn before the app was built, not after, and it is published here already.
The 1 as a mask

Photography cut into the numeral.

This door’s loudest move, and the one thing it does that no other door does. A photograph is cut into the shape of the 1 rather than set beside it, so the mark and the picture are the same object.

  • The shape is the published file. every1-numeral.svg, never a redrawing and never a font character. It is a mask, so it takes no ink and is never recoloured.
  • The photograph still obeys every rule. Documentary capture, consent before the shutter, a caption where it is published. A shape does not exempt a picture.
  • It holds a photograph and nothing else. Never type, never a logo, never another mark. No outline, no shadow, no rotation.
  • One per view. Used twice on a page it stops being a signature and becomes a pattern.

For everyone outside the team

This door has its own front door.

Partner organisations carrying EVERY1 at a conference do not need this guide, which is written for the house. They need the mark, the room it wants, and the short list of things that break it. That is published on its own domain at brand.every1movement.com, built from this same repository so the two cannot disagree, and carrying its own copies of everything so nobody is ever sent to the parent’s domain to fetch a logo.

A new country

Drawn, not generated.

A country lockup carries its country’s name as outlined type, so the build cannot compose one. Each is drawn.

  1. They come from the source file. New country lockups are made in the EVERY1 Canva file, canva.link/saa5nitous3vxts, which is where every mark on this page came from.
  2. The bar carries the country’s flag. Its colours are the flag’s, which is the recorded exception to the six-colour palette. USA is the single exception to the exception: its bar is Flame, White and Midnight, because the flag is close enough that a true red and blue reads as a mistake.
  3. A segment must survive its ground. A black or Midnight segment disappears on Midnight. On a dark ground it lifts, and the published files already do this.
  4. It arrives through the drop zone. Put the export in assets/logos/_inbox/, promote it, and it is cut in three inks with its clear space and minimum size recorded. Email brand@theword.world.

The mark

One word, and the 1 that leaves it.

Heavy caps with the numeral drawn as its own shape, standing at a quarter again the cap height of EVERY and carrying the only Flame on the mark. The 1 is the same drawing in every form, which is what lets it leave the wordmark and still be the movement. Every form is vector, so it scales without limit and recolours by changing one fill value.

Set in DM Sans at weight 900, converted to outlines. The mark is artwork, not live text, so it never needs the font installed and never reflows. Do not re-set it in another face, do not stretch it, and do not redraw the numeral by hand. No form carries an endorsement line and none should be added: this door stands on its own, which is the recorded exception and the reason the mark reads the way it does. If a size or colour you need does not exist, request it.

Rules that differ from the parent

Where this door departs.

Everything in the Brand Guide applies here. These are the points where EVERY1 Movement is deliberately not the parent.

  • Midnight ground, full bleed. This is the only door that runs dark by default. Footage fills the frame wherever the layout allows.
  • Flame to the full tenth. The parent's ceiling, used fully. It still never carries text: Ember does that job.
  • Most footage, least chrome. Motion carries this door. Where a still would do for CLEAN or TRAIN, EVERY1 uses the clip.
  • This door is the recorded exception. EVERY1 carries no endorsement line and no parent lockup, on the YouVersion model: the movement, the app, and the activation platform are joined by people who have not yet heard of the house behind them. Law III stands for every other door. Nothing else about this guide is waived.
