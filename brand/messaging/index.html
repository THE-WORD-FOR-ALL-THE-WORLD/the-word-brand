01 · How To Use This GuideThe Word · Messaging Guide

One guide. Every voice.

This document is written for everyone who speaks or writes for THE WORD FOR ALL THE WORLD: leadership, team members, volunteers, and outside writers, designers, and agencies. It assumes no tribal knowledge. If you have never worked with us before, this guide is enough.

The Brand Guide (v4.4) governs how we look. This guide governs what we say. The two are companions: Law II (everything is a record), Law III (one house, named front doors), and Law IV (the institution signs its work) apply to words exactly as they apply to design.

The rule of rule: where wording in any older document, website, deck, or social post conflicts with this guide, this guide wins. Changes to this guide happen one way: proposed in writing, approved by Joel and Nathan, recorded in the changelog (§14).

05 · What We Are, What We Are NotThe Word · Messaging Guide

Say what we are.
Correct what we're not.

The name THE WORD FOR ALL THE WORLD invites assumptions. Messaging corrects them plainly, without being defensive: one clear sentence and move on.

We Are
  • A Spirit-led ministry that strengthens local churches to do the Great Commission.
  • A servant of the local church: we come to serve their house, not build ours.
  • Evangelists and trainers: we preach, we stir, we equip, and we leave the church stronger than we found it.
We Are Not
  • Not a Bible translation ministry. "THE WORD" is the Word preached, not the Word translated.
  • Not a denomination. We plant fire, not flags.
  • Not a replacement for the local church. If there is no local church to partner with in an area, we hold off. Our goal is never to create spiritual orphans.

How one weekend tells the whole story

We arrive in a city Thursday or Friday. Evenings are Revival To My City services: worship, preaching, altar calls, healing, testimonies captured. Hearts get cleaned out by the power of God. Saturday morning is the EVERY1 interest meeting: what the movement is, how to join, and the first outreach. Participants personally evangelize in the community that same morning. From there, they sign up for the School of the Local Church, starting with the free personal evangelism course. One person can be in all three at once, and often is.

10 · Voice & VocabularyThe Word · Messaging Guide

Bold, clear,
simple, direct.

Pastoral with prophetic urgency. Invitation-first. Short, punchy sentences. Never polished-corporate. Never hype. Never churchy. If a new believer couldn't understand it, rewrite it.

Would Nathan actually say this? Does it point people to Jesus Himself? Is it clear to a new believer? Does it invite response?
If unsure, simplify and center Jesus.

Words we carry

This recurring language is our fingerprint. Use these phrases exactly. They are load-bearing.

Words we never use

Three ban categories, with starter lists. These lists are proposals for the v1.0 review: strike or keep each one. Theological words (repentance, salvation, Holy Spirit baptism) are never banned; they are explained. The test is always the filter: clear to a new believer.

Hype
insane · epic · unreal · next-level · game-changer · "God showed up and showed out" · "you won't believe" · walls of 🔥 emojis · ALL-CAPS excitement
Hype manufactures a feeling. We report what God actually did. The record is more powerful than the adjective.
Corporate Polish
leverage · synergy · solutions · optimize · engage stakeholders · "excited to announce" · comprehensive · robust
We are a ministry, not a vendor. Corporate language makes the guide the hero and the mission a product.
Churchy Insider Talk
traveling mercies · hedge of protection · "love on" · "do life together" · "press in" · "contend for breakthrough" · "a God thing" · "fresh anointing" (unexplained) · "blessed to announce"
Insider language tells a new believer this isn't for them. Everything we say is for them.

Rewrites · before and after

Never thisAlways this
"🔥🔥 INSANE night!! God showed UP and showed OUT!!""Friday night in [city]: [n] people heard the gospel. [n] turned their hearts back to Jesus. The fire is spreading." (numbers from the official record only)
"Join us as we press into a fresh anointing and contend for breakthrough.""Come. Jesus is moving. Bring the person you've been praying for."
"We provide comprehensive discipleship solutions for local church partners.""We train the local church to walk with Jesus."

Standing rules

Short sentences. One idea each. Say Jesus by name, not "faith," not "spirituality." Never invent a statistic, a quote, or a testimony; the record or nothing (Law II). Never manufacture a catchphrase: if a line has to be explained to be catchy, kill it. Invitation before information: every piece ends with something the reader can do.

12 · Proof & Testimony PolicyThe Word · Messaging Guide

The record is
our only proof.

This guide contains no statistics on purpose. Numbers live in one place, the official ministry record, and messaging cites that record rather than freezing figures that will age. Per the Minister Agreement: official numbers only, never estimates, never memory. A guide that hardcodes stats violates Law II the day the record updates; a guide that points to the record never does.

Testimonies · the four slots

Every testimony is a record of what God did: real, consented, and named. Never fabricated, never composited, never anonymous.

Testimony · The Record of What God Did
Before
Life as it was, in the person's own words.
Encounter
The moment Jesus intervened: where and when.
Transformation
What changed: specific, verifiable, honest.
Outcome
Life now, and the invitation to respond.
