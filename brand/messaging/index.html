Section 01How to use this standard

This is the one messaging document. Where wording anywhere else conflicts with it, this document rules.

Four levels of authority

Not every line here carries the same weight. Each section is marked with the level that governs it, and the level decides what it takes to change it.

LevelWhat it covers, and how it changes
LockedDoctrine, purpose, mission, vision and foundational convictions. Changes only through formal leadership approval, recorded in the changelog with two signers.
StandardDefault voice, terminology and communication practice. Depart only for a clear audience or ministry reason, and say what the reason was.
FlexibleIllustrations, expressions, calls to action and channel-specific execution. Change freely inside the laws above.
ExperimentalCampaign language or profiles being tested, not yet part of the permanent standard. Usable now, not yet binding.

A locked item cannot be quietly redefined by a team member, a contractor or an AI system. The rules that enforce that live at /governance, with the rest of this ministry's policies, processes and procedures.

Founder-source authority

The founder's vision writings are primary interpretive material for this standard. Their theology, images and recurring phrases govern later summaries and campaign language. Editorial work may clarify grammar, correct references, distinguish internal language from public language and organise the material. It must never replace the heart or the theological intent of the source.

Before you write anything

Identify the speaking brand, the audience, the relationship, the channel, the desired action and the theological guardrails that apply. Use this standard as governing context, not as inspiration. Then check the finished piece against the publication audit before it goes anywhere.

CLEAN

Purpose. Stirring the local church to return to her first love. (Revelation 2:4)

Theological foundation. CLEAN is built on the revelation of Mark 4: the sower sows THE WORD, but how that Word affects a person's heart depends upon the condition of the soil. CLEAN prepares the heart so the Word can take root and produce.

Soil diagnostics and ministry response

SoilCondition, and the response it requires
PathwayThose who are not born again and need to receive Christ Jesus as Lord and Savior. The solution is salvation: the stony heart is removed, a heart of flesh is received, and the Word can be planted.
StonyBelievers whose hearts contain offense, bitterness, unforgiveness, unbelief or traditions that prevent the Word from taking root. The solution is teaching that establishes the truth and produces stability.
ThornyThose who love the Word but nurture competing cares, the deceitfulness of riches, the lust of other things or idols of attention. The solution is the fire of the Holy Spirit and the fear of the Lord, burning away what chokes the Word.
GoodBelievers actively applying the Word. The thirtyfold, sixtyfold and hundredfold harvest reveals increasing fruitfulness, and the degree of increase is related to the degree of surrender.

CLEAN messaging law. CLEAN does not shame people for the condition of their soil. It identifies what prevents the Word from producing, declares the appropriate truth, and calls the heart back to Jesus. Its emotional movement is hunger, repentance, freedom and readiness.

Revival To My City

Revival To My City is the principal in-person expression of CLEAN and the entry point into the full process. It invites people through worship and revival meetings, then expands their understanding of revival from a meeting they attend into a mandate they carry.

Public language must never suggest deception or a bait and switch. Internally the intent may be described as flipping the concept of revival on its head. Externally, say clearly that the gathering moves from encounter to mobilisation.

Common phrases. "Lighting hearts and seats on fire for Jesus." "You can have a real relationship with Jesus."

BURN

Purpose. Empowering the local church to walk in unity, do the Great Commission and walk in God's calling. (Acts 2:17)

Theological foundation. BURN is built on the holy desire of the Spirit within us. When we yield our lives to the Spirit of God, a burning comes that cannot be silenced. We inspire men and women to burn for Jesus and impact the nations.

Unity and the common command. Burning gets people on the same page. Jesus sent the seventy with the same mission and gave direction concerning what to do, where to go and how to do it. Today Jesus still desires His Church to be directed, governed and trained to burn for Him everywhere she goes. Unity brings power. We unite cities behind the common cause of GO YE.

Sphere-language guardrail. These are Kingdom-purpose statements. They are not endorsements of a political party, an economic ideology or a personality. We communicate the believer's assignment within each sphere without allowing partisan controversy to replace the Gospel.

The EVERY1 Movement

The EVERY1 Movement is the global soul-winning expression of BURN. It unites everyday believers, churches and ministries around one common mission: EVERY1 in the Church for EVERY1 outside the Church. Its horizon is every tribe, every tongue, every nation and every one-on-one interaction for Jesus.

The mandate. The Great Commission is for EVERY1, not only pastors, evangelists, the most holy or the already polished. EVERY1 calls people to accept Christ, accept His commission, build commissioned habits and train others. Salvation immediately opens into mission: what Jesus has done in us is carried to the person in front of us.

Certainty by knowing. EVERY1 does not organise its message around uncertainty. It brings certainty through knowing: knowing Jesus, knowing the pure and simple Gospel, and knowing His command to GO. A believer does not need to understand every method before obeying what Jesus has made clear. The Body then helps each person grow in truth, maturity and effectiveness as each part does its own special work. (Ephesians 4:12–13, 16)

This does not celebrate doctrinal error or careless ministry. It refuses to make polished expertise a prerequisite for simple Gospel obedience. Action on the Word produces hunger, hunger leads people deeper into Christ, and the Church equips them toward maturity.

The operating revelation. Ephesians 4:12–13, 16 provides the governing picture: Christ equips the Body, every part does its own special work, and the whole Body grows together in love. EVERY1 joins fire to position. Fire supplies power. Position supplies direction. Unity supplies multiplication. Systems supply sustainability. The Gospel and the Great Commission remain the controlling mission.

Taking position is not platform-seeking or self-appointment. It is accepting and stewarding the assignment God has given a believer within government, business, influence, family, ministry, medicine, or an intersection of those spheres. No sphere is the whole mission. Each part strengthens the others, and together they sustain the mission of reaching people with the Gospel.

The main thing. "We function in keeping the main thing the main thing." We function to promote the Great Commission and the winning of souls through the mandate of Jesus. Every sphere, system, ministry relationship and movement activity must serve that purpose. The spheres do not replace evangelism. They position the whole Body to carry, protect, resource, communicate, follow up and multiply the work of the Great Commission.

EVERY1 is designed for interworking among churches and ministries. Agreement around the essential Gospel and the Great Commission allows us to work together in soul winning without requiring uniformity on every secondary doctrine. In plain internal language: if we agree on the big stuff, you are welcome to take part.

Open, but not muted. This openness is not spiritual neutrality. EVERY1 is openly Spirit-led and continuationist. We expect the present ministry, gifts, leading and power of the Holy Spirit. Prayer for healing, deliverance, supernatural ministry and bold dependence upon the Spirit are part of the movement's normal environment, not optional language hidden to make cooperation easier.

We will not yield our passion for Jesus or the fire of the Holy Spirit for institutional acceptance, broader participation or greater comfort. We do not reduce unity to the least controversial message everyone can tolerate. A person, church or ministry does not have to mirror every expression or secondary conviction of THE WORD to cooperate. They must, however, understand the environment they are entering and respect Spirit-led ministry rather than oppose, suppress or continually contest it. We are not exclusive, but we will not shy away from the Holy Spirit.

Movement-level communication stays focused on the pure and simple Gospel, Spirit-led ministry, prayer, testimony, action, follow-up and mobilisation. It does not become a forum for arguing secondary doctrine, promoting a personality, recruiting people away from their local church, or advancing one participating ministry over another. Unity is not doctrinal carelessness: questions that affect the integrity of the Gospel, the work of the Holy Spirit, ethical conduct, safety or accountability must be addressed. Deeper doctrinal formation and pastoral care remain rooted in the local church.

The global horizon. EVERY1 is as personal as a conversation with a coworker and as large as a nation. Individual obedience replicates through communication, community and example until the knowledge of the Lord reaches from the least to the greatest. (Hebrews 8:11) For the earth will be filled with the knowledge of the glory of the Lord, as the waters cover the sea. Habakkuk 2:14 NKJV

The movement participates in the worldwide Gospel witness that precedes the Second Coming of Christ, and looks toward the final outpouring of God, the fullest expression of what most people mean when they speak of revival. And this gospel of the kingdom will be preached in all the world as a witness to all the nations, and then the end will come. Matthew 24:14 NKJV

The system that sustains revival. Revival produces fire. EVERY1 provides Spirit-led structure that helps the Church sustain, direct and multiply that fire through specific acts of Gospel obedience. The system does not control the Holy Spirit. It channels the power and passion He places in believers into visible avenues of soul winning, follow-up, testimony, unity and continued action. EVERY1 moves people from spectatorship into participation: instead of seeing what God is doing somewhere else and wishing it would happen in their city, believers receive a clear pathway to act where they are.

Methodology. Accept Christ, accept the Commission, instill commissioned habits, train others.

Core participation loop. Accept the mission, do the mission, track what happened, see it on the feed, get inspired, repeat. Growth is pictured as multiplying fire: match, kindling, twigs, sticks, logs, wet logs, trees, forests, mountain ranges, the world.

Movement and app. The movement is larger than the app. EVERY1 is the people, the mandate, the culture and the worldwide action. The app is the supporting system that helps the movement track, communicate, inspire and equip at scale. In plain English: this is an app that makes preaching the Gospel fun and cool. It is built for the everyday believer and the wider Church, not as a promotional app for THE WORD. Ownership may be stated transparently, but the experience serves the mission rather than continually drawing attention back to the founding ministry.

EVERY1 messaging law. EVERY1 sounds high-energy, active, certain, global and inspiring. Lead with the mandate, the person in front of us, and the opportunity to act now. Use verbs: accept, GO, preach, reach, pray, do, track, connect, celebrate, repeat, train. Do not make confusion, reluctance or lack of permission the controlling emotional idea. EVERY1 supplies a clear mission and a path for action. Deeper formation belongs primarily to TRAIN.

Section 07Voice DNA

Voice attributes. Passionate. Personal. Apostolic. Practical. Authoritative.

Governing voice statement. We sound full of faith, burning for Jesus, driven by love, and Spirit-led in our conversations.

The filter

Before anything is published, it has to pass this:

Does this point people to Jesus Himself? Is every claim in it something we can show? Is it clear to a new believer? Does it ask for one thing? If the answer is unsure anywhere, simplify and centre Jesus.

The filter is deliberately not "would Joel say this" or "would Nathan say this." THE WORD has its own voice, and it is not a blend of its leaders. When a named leader is speaking, Section 19 governs instead.

Standing rules

Short sentences. One idea each. Say Jesus by name, not "faith," not "spirituality." Never invent a statistic, a quote, or a testimony; the record or nothing (Law II). Never manufacture a catchphrase: if a line has to be explained to be catchy, kill it. Invitation before information: every piece ends with something the reader can do.

Presence before platformWe speak as people who have ministered to Jesus before speaking for Him.
Declare, do not argueWe speak with confidence, without defensiveness, desperation or needless persuasion.
Faith is the atmosphereOur confidence comes from God's Word, and our urgency comes from love for people.
Say it plainlyWe do not conceal our beliefs to appear acceptable.
One messageEvery communication has one primary assignment.
Conviction without condemnationWe expose lies in order to replace them with truth, not to define people by failure.
Authority without self-importanceWe speak with certainty about Jesus and humility about ourselves.
Excitement from substanceWe do not manufacture emotional intensity through exaggeration.
Numbers are stewardshipResults belong on the record, but they do not prove our worth or our anointing.
Produce movementPeople should know what is true, why it matters, and what obedient action to take.

We do not sound

Religious or stale. Clichéd. Manipulative. Polished but empty. Politically partisan. Hype-driven. Full of ourselves.

Section 08Audience and relational posture

This is the master-brand audience priority, in order. When a piece has to choose, it chooses the audience nearer the top.

1. Ministry partners and donors

Posture: fellow laborers in a shared assignment. Partners are not a means to an end. They are part of the mission. Everything in Section 11 governs how we speak to them.

2. Institutions, governments and public leaders

Posture: apostolic leader and credible institutional partner. Speak with permanence and verifiable record. This is the audience for which the reporting standard in Section 14 exists.

3. Pastors and church leaders

Posture: trusted ministry partner. We come to serve their house, not build ours. When we leave, their people stay, on fire and equipped.

4. Evangelists and ministers

Posture: apostolic leader and fellow laborer. Speak to shared assignment and shared fire, without competing for the same platform.

5. The ordinary believer

Posture: teacher and equipper. For EVERY1 participants specifically, the posture shifts: a movement they actively belong to through shared mission, not an audience being addressed.

6. The spiritually hungry but passive believer

Posture: teacher and equipper, calling them out of the pew. They know there must be more but have become dry, distracted or stuck. Speak to the hunger, never to the failure.

7. Those who do not yet know Jesus

Posture: personal, loving and fatherly invitation. Section 10 governs the language.

Fatherly language

Fatherly language is present primarily in Joel's personal voice, and in discipleship or pastoral communication. It supports the institutional voice. It does not define it.

Section 11Partnership language

Locked

The partnership law. We do not extract money from partners. We invite fellow laborers into a God-given assignment, steward their trust, communicate frequently, and faithfully report the fruit.

Governing statements

  • Partnership is our strength.
  • Partners are not a means to an end. They are part of the mission itself.
  • Partners are worked with and invested in, not managed.
  • Partners participate with us in a shared assignment from God.
  • Your giving produces glory to God.
  • Partnership is a two-way street. We pray, invest and believe with partners for their lives, ministries, businesses and families to produce results for Jesus.

The giving rule

Do not ask a partner to give unless the communication is specifically part of a defined giving campaign. Ordinary partner communication must not contain a routine financial ask. A partner communication should remain valuable even if the reader never gives.

Frictionless giving

Make donations frictionless when a partner wants to give. The path should be easy, secure, clear, accessible and transparent. Frictionless giving is infrastructure, not persuasion: a visible giving link, a simple form, clear designation options, a prompt receipt and accessible help may remain available at all times without turning ordinary partner communication into fundraising. Do not hide how to give, do not create unnecessary steps, and do not make a willing giver search for the process.

The Philippians foundation

The governing wisdom for partnership communication is drawn from Philippians. Partner communication should make a meaningful point rooted in biblical truth and wisdom. It must not become a stream of generic organisational updates.

Partnership laws

  • The people and partners are not a means to further the mission. They are part of the mission.
  • The primary action is investment: communicate truth, wisdom, prayer, encouragement, correction, clarity or commissioning that produces value in the person.
  • Pray for partners whenever they come to mind, and intentionally include them in the ministry's prayer life.
  • Pray that their love overflows, that they grow in knowledge and understanding, understand what truly matters, live pure lives and produce the fruit of salvation.
  • Cherish partners. They have a special place in the ministry's heart and must never be valued according to financial contribution.
  • Communicate so partners can grow and experience the joy of their faith.
  • Share what God is doing so the ministry and its partners can rejoice together in Jesus.
  • Encourage, correct, invest, update, commission and safeguard faith as the relationship and situation require.
  • Write to the actual people and needs involved. Do not send the same generic letter when different partners need different ministry.
  • Communicate frequently enough to steward trust, but never create communication merely to fill a schedule.
  • Listen and report back. Partnership includes learning how a church, ministry, business, family or person is doing, and identifying ways to strengthen them.
  • When appropriate, send trusted people to visit, encourage, help, and return with an accurate report.
  • Celebrate what partners have invested without making them responsible for sustaining the ministry.
  • Giving must arise from their joy, conviction and participation in the assignment, not from guilt, pressure or implied obligation.

Prohibited fundraising practices

  • Guilt, shame, fear or artificial deadlines
  • Implying God's work will fail unless a donor rescues it
  • Promising financial return, healing, miracles, favor or access in exchange for a gift
  • Exaggerated or unverified outcomes
  • Valuing people according to how much they give
  • Using urgency to bypass prayer or wise stewardship

The partner communication pattern

  1. Begin with sincere encouragement or gratitude.
  2. Communicate one biblical truth, point of wisdom, or needed correction.
  3. Invest that truth directly into the partner's life, family, ministry, business or assignment.
  4. Update them on what God is doing, when the update strengthens faith or shared understanding.
  5. Acknowledge their prayer, participation, service or giving appropriately.
  6. Invite prayer, response, celebration or action consistent with the point of the message.
  7. Include a financial invitation only when the communication is an explicit giving campaign.
  8. Close with prayer, blessing, affection, or a clear expression of continued relationship.

The partnership team

The prayer team belongs within the partnership function. Anyone serving in partnership communication should understand the partnership teaching in Philippians. Promotion and responsibility within partnership communication require a demonstrated, genuine concern for the welfare of partners, not merely writing skill, fundraising performance or administrative efficiency.

Section 13Theological language

Locked

The Gospel

The Gospel is the good news that you can have a real relationship with God. People often do not desire that relationship because they do not know God's character.

That is the ministry's governing description of the Gospel. It is not a substitute for a complete presentation of the plan of salvation. A complete Gospel presentation includes sin, repentance, Jesus' identity, His cross, His resurrection, faith, confession of His lordship, forgiveness, the new birth, eternal life, and the beginning of a real relationship with Him.

How we present Jesus

Jesus Christ is the Son of God, Lord, Savior and King. He died for our sins, rose from the dead, reigns today, and is coming again. His identity and work may also be proclaimed as crucified and risen Redeemer, Healer, Baptizer in the Holy Spirit, Deliverer, Bridegroom and Friend, without reducing Him to sentiment, self-help, or a ticket to Heaven.

Descriptions to avoid or qualify

  • Jesus is my buddy.
  • Jesus is your life coach.
  • Jesus only wants you to be happy.
  • Jesus is a ticket to Heaven.
  • Jesus is just waiting for you to let Him help.
  • "Jesus loves you and has a wonderful plan," when separated from sin, the cross, repentance, resurrection and lordship.

A real relationship with Jesus

Relationship begins at the new birth. Fellowship is cultivated through the Word, repentance, obedience and walking with the Holy Spirit. It includes being born again, knowing His character through His Word, receiving His righteousness, prayer, worship, repentance, obedience and fellowship with Him.

It produces love, holiness, confidence before God, spiritual fruit, renewed thinking, victory over sin and deception, compassion for people, and participation in Christ's mission.

It is not merely church attendance, religious language, an emotional experience, repeating a prayer, intellectual agreement, or viewing salvation only as a ticket to Heaven.

Maturity appears as a person recognises God's voice through His Word, obeys His will, quickly repents, walks in love, stewards authority responsibly, produces lasting fruit, and represents Jesus to others.

The Holy Spirit

The Holy Spirit is God, and a Person. He speaks, leads, reveals truth, brings Scripture to remembrance, and empowers believers for life, witness and ministry.

Every believer can and should be filled with the Holy Spirit. Speaking in tongues is available to every believer and is the initial physical evidence of the baptism in the Holy Spirit. The gifts of the Spirit are active today. Signs, wonders, healing, prophecy and supernatural ministry should be normal within the Church.

"The Holy Spirit runs our services" means we prepare diligently while giving Him final authority over the direction, timing, message and ministry of a service. It does not excuse disorder, lack of preparation, or lack of accountability.

Holy Spirit language to avoid

  • Referring to the Holy Spirit as "it"
  • Speaking of Him only as a force, energy, atmosphere or power source
  • Saying "we use the Holy Spirit"
  • Manipulative uses of "the Holy Spirit told me"
  • Mystical language that cannot be explained from Scripture
  • Treating emotional intensity as automatic proof that the Spirit is moving
  • Saying someone does not have the Holy Spirit merely because they have not received the baptism in the Holy Spirit

Doctrinal disagreement

Joel Zimmer

Assignment. Joel is sent into the local Church to strengthen believers through the Word and the demonstration of the Holy Spirit, awaken hunger for Jesus, and help people grow into their place in the Body of Christ.

A prophetic word Joel received likened his assignment to Elisha pouring salt into bitter water and making it sweet: "I'll pour you out into the Church and the world." This is a remembered paraphrase. It must not be presented as a verbatim quotation until it has been checked against the original wording.

Relational posture. A seasoned fellow laborer and pastoral teacher with fatherly warmth. He speaks as someone who has walked with Jesus, received from spiritual mentors, tested what he has heard by Scripture, and now wants others to know what is available to them in Christ.

Voice DNA. Relational, testimonial, Scripture-first, warm, steady, plainspoken, full of faith. Joel often begins with something Jesus showed him or someone demonstrated to him, walks through a Bible narrative, draws out a memorable principle, and invites the hearer to believe, receive, speak and act on the Word. Repetition establishes truth. It does not manufacture emotion.

Recurring messages. The Church age is God's mystery on the earth. This is the dispensation of grace and the age of the Holy Spirit. Believe, speak and act on the Word through the Holy Spirit. You are a three-part being: spirit, soul and body. Grow into maturity and take your place in the Body of Christ.

Emotional destination. People should feel known, settled, refreshed, full of faith, and ready to mature in what God has spoken.

Signature language. "Open the Word." "Believe it, speak it and walk it out." "The presence of the Holy Spirit." "All is well." Use these when they naturally fit the message. Do not force all of them into every communication.

Joel sounds like a trusted minister opening the Bible at the kitchen table; a psalmist carrying the Word through music; a mentor who honors those who demonstrated Christ to him; a teacher who expects the Word to work; a pastor who values the presence of the Holy Spirit more than an atmosphere.

Joel does not sound like corporate, hurried, theatrical, self-promotional, controlling, or dependent on atmosphere rather than the presence of God. He does not replace Scripture with personality or tradition, and he does not use fatherly authority to control people.

Music and ministry. Music carries the Word, creates hunger for Jesus, and ministers refreshment through the Holy Spirit. All Is Well is part of this identity. Governing expression: "It creates a hunger within the people that only God can fill."

Synthesized sample, not a quotation: Open the Word. Let the Holy Spirit show you what belongs to you in Christ. Believe it, speak it and walk it out. You are a spirit; you have a soul; and you live in a body. As you stay with Jesus and act on His Word, He will mature you and bring you into your place in the Body. All is well.

Nathan Zimmer

Assignment. Nathan is sent into the local Church to send the local Church into the world, mobilising believers to preach the Gospel, win souls, and live the Great Commission.

Relational posture. An apostolic and evangelistic mobiliser with prophetic urgency. He invites people first, and confronts passivity when necessary. He speaks to believers as people who are called, anointed and able to act, not as spectators waiting for a title or perfect understanding.

Voice DNA. Bold, clear, simple, direct, high-energy, Spirit-led. Short declarations carry conviction, teaching supplies biblical certainty, systems give passion an avenue, and the message ends in movement. He brings authority without arrogance, fire without manipulation, urgency without artificial pressure.

Recurring messages. You can have a real relationship with Jesus. Every believer, not just the fivefold ministry, is called to preach the Gospel and win souls. Spirit-led systems help every part do its part and sustain revival.

Emotional destination. People should feel awakened, certain, empowered, responsible, and ready to GO.

Signature language. "Lighting hearts and seats on fire for Jesus." "Fire starts fire." "You can have a real relationship with Jesus." "The Great Commission is for all, not just those proved faithful." "Every tribe, every tongue, every nation and every one-on-one interaction will be for Jesus." Preserve the urgency and the play on EVERY1, without turning every message into a slogan.

Nathan sounds like a ministry leader calling the Church to GO; a psalmist who can preach through a song; a builder who turns revelation into a repeatable pathway; an evangelistic leader who makes the next act of obedience clear; a voice that insists the Holy Spirit will never lose His value.

Nathan does not sound like passive, vague, polished-corporate, academically abstract, hype-driven, manipulative, or afraid of the Holy Spirit. He does not create urgency through shame, make methods the source of power, or let doctrinal side debates displace the mandate of Jesus.

Music and ministry. Music is another means of preaching the Gospel: a psalmist expression that carries people toward Jesus, faith, mission and the world. Governing expression: "Preach my songs and sing my sermons." Music is supporting and situational rather than the centre of Nathan's leadership identity.

Synthesized sample, not a quotation: You can have a real relationship with Jesus, and every believer is called to carry His Gospel. The Great Commission is for all, not just the fivefold and not just those considered proved faithful. Let the Holy Spirit light your heart on fire, take the next person in front of you, and GO. Fire starts fire.

Both leaders are Scripture-first, Spirit-led, full of faith, centred on a real relationship with Jesus, committed to the local Church, and unwilling to separate the Great Commission from the power of the Holy Spirit. Both may preach, teach, lead, sing, invite response and speak with authority. Their difference is emphasis, not opposition. Joel is generally sent into the local Church through testimony, teaching, mentorship, worship and maturation. Nathan is generally sent into the local Church to send the local Church into the world through mandate, mobilisation, systems, music and action. These are governing tendencies, not rigid limits.

Section 20Before and after

Each comparison teaches a governing law. The off-brand language is illustrative and must never be reused as ministry copy. Diagnose the failure before copying the on-brand pattern.

Public social post

Never"The world is dying while lazy Christians sit in church. Stop making excuses and do something."

Contempt and shame as motivation, an unsupported generalisation, and a believer left condemned rather than commissioned.

Always"You were saved and sent. Someone in your world needs to know what Jesus has done for you. Ask the Holy Spirit who your 1 is, begin the conversation, and GO."

Revival To My City invitation

Never"Revival is coming! Do not miss the most life-changing event your city has ever seen."

Hype, an outcome promise that cannot be guaranteed, and revival treated primarily as an event.

Always"There must be more than going through the motions. Revival To My City is calling the local church to return to her first love. Come worship, receive the Word, and make room for the Holy Spirit to awaken what has become dry or distracted. Attend Revival in [City]."

EVERY1 Movement

Never"Not sure whether you are allowed to evangelize? Download our ministry app and learn how."

Uncertainty as the controlling emotion, the app before the movement, and EVERY1 reduced to another content platform.

Always"You were saved and sent. The Great Commission belongs to EVERY1. Join believers, churches and ministries around the world who are preaching the Gospel in the power of the Holy Spirit. Accept the mission. Reach your 1. Then GO again."

School of the Local Church

Never"An elite leadership intensive for serious Christians ready to reach the next level."

Exclusive, self-improving and status-driven. It never names relationship with Jesus, authority in Christ, or service within the local church.

Always"You were not called to remain unsure of who you are, what Jesus has given you, or where you belong. The School of the Local Church trains believers to know their authority in Christ, build a real relationship with Jesus, and take their place in the local church. Begin Training."

Ordinary partner communication

Never"Because you are one of our most faithful donors, we wanted to tell you about another urgent financial need."

It values the person according to giving, turns routine communication into fundraising, and treats the relationship as a pipeline to money.

Always"We remembered you in prayer today. We are asking the Lord to strengthen your faith, family, work and assignment. Here is a truth that has been helping our team remain full of faith this month, and we believe it will strengthen you too."

Explicit giving campaign

Never"If you do not give by midnight, this opportunity may disappear. Sow today and expect your miracle."

Pressure, artificial urgency, and a promised spiritual return.

Always"We are inviting partners to help provide [verified need] for [specific assignment]. The current confirmed cost is [amount], and gifts designated to this campaign will be used for [purpose]. Please pray and participate only as the Lord leads you. If you choose to give, the process is secure and simple: [link]. We will report what was received, how it was used, and the verified fruit."

Formal ministry report

Never"Our campaign was a massive success with thousands of lives changed and unprecedented miracles."

Vague superlatives, numbers treated as proof of spiritual success, and no evidence classification.

Always"From [dates], the team served in [locations]. Attendance was [counting method]. Ministry records document [verified number] Gospel responses, [verified number] follow-up connections, and [verified number] reported healings. Testimonies still awaiting confirmation are labelled unverified. These results are reported as fruit entrusted to us, not as the measure of our spiritual success."

Speaking about the Church

Never"The Church is asleep, compromised and failing its generation."

Accusation as the destination, no pastoral love, condemnation instead of a pathway back to Jesus.

Always"Many believers know there must be more but have become dry, distracted or weighed down. We speak truthfully about what prevents the Word from producing, while calling the Church back to her first love. The destination is never shame. It is awakening, responsibility and action."

Speaking to someone who does not know Jesus

Never"The lost and unsaved need to get right with God before it is too late."

It labels the person before establishing love, assumes religious vocabulary, and leads with condemnation.

Always"If you do not yet know Jesus, He is not distant or unavailable. He knows you, loves you, and is inviting you to receive forgiveness, new life, and a real relationship with Him."

Preaching-derived material

NeverSource: "when Jesus calls you you go you do not wait until everything makes sense you go". Edited to: "Spiritual progress requires embracing uncertainty and proactively pursuing your divine purpose."

It replaces the preacher's words, cadence, force and theology with generic editorial language.

AlwaysPunctuation only: "When Jesus calls you, you GO. You do not wait until everything makes sense. You GO."

Cross-ministry unity and the Holy Spirit

Never"To ensure everyone feels comfortable, EVERY1 avoids disputed supernatural subjects and focuses on shared values."

It creates unity by muting the Holy Spirit, and turns the movement spiritually neutral.

Always"EVERY1 welcomes cooperation around the essential Gospel and the Great Commission. We are not exclusive, but we will not shy away from the Holy Spirit. Spirit-led ministry, healing, deliverance and supernatural dependence upon Him remain part of our normal environment."

Section 21The publication audit

Locked

The publication rule. A message is not ready because it is grammatically correct or visually polished. It is ready when it is faithful to this ministry's heart, theology, audience, evidence and assigned action. Any failed doctrinal, Scripture, integrity or partnership item must be corrected before publication.

Identity and mission

  • Does the message strengthen the local church rather than compete with it?
  • Does it ultimately support a real relationship with Jesus, the Great Commission, or ministry to Him?
  • Is the correct brand or initiative speaking?
  • Does it preserve CLEAN, BURN, TRAIN rather than confusing the roles of the initiatives?

Audience and posture

  • Is the primary audience unmistakable?
  • Are we speaking to the person directly, unless the content is intentionally informative or formal?
  • Is our relationship to the audience correct: apostolic leader, trusted partner, fellow laborer, teacher, or movement?
  • Does the reader leave loved, strengthened and responsible, rather than shamed, manipulated or diminished?

Voice and language

  • Does this sound authoritative, warm, direct, Spirit-led and full of faith?
  • Is the language plain and globally understandable without weakening the truth?
  • Are sentences active, concrete, and appropriately energetic for the channel?
  • Have vague ministry clichés, hype, corporate jargon and artificial urgency been removed?
  • Are Church, local church, Gospel, Scripture, Holy Spirit, Kingdom and EVERY1 styled per Section 16.9?
  • Are there any em dashes? There must not be.

Gospel, Church and Holy Spirit

  • If this is a complete Gospel presentation, are sin, repentance, the cross, resurrection, lordship, forgiveness, new birth, relationship with Jesus and eternal life represented faithfully?
  • Is Jesus presented as He is, not reduced to inspiration, sentiment or self-improvement?
  • Does language about people who do not know Jesus remain personal, clear, hopeful and accurate?
  • When correcting the Church, is the destination repentance, awakening and action rather than accusation or shame?
  • Have we avoided muting, apologising for, or compartmentalising the Holy Spirit?
  • Is supernatural ministry presented without hype, spectacle, or promises God has not made?

EVERY1 Movement

  • Does EVERY1 sound high-energy, active, certain, global and inspiring?
  • Does it begin with Christ's mandate rather than uncertainty about permission?
  • Does it keep the controlling statement intact: "We function in keeping the main thing the main thing"?
  • Is the movement clearly larger than the app, and does the app serve participation rather than promote THE WORD?
  • Are people moved from spectatorship to action: accept, GO, preach, reach, pray, do, track, connect, celebrate, repeat, train?
  • Does sphere language help each person take their position while keeping soul winning central?
  • Does cross-ministry unity allow secondary differences without becoming spiritually neutral?

Partnership and giving

  • Does the communication invest value, wisdom, prayer or encouragement into the partner?
  • Would it remain valuable if the reader never gave?
  • Are partners treated as fellow laborers and part of the mission, rather than a source of money?
  • Is any financial invitation limited to an explicit giving campaign?
  • Is the giving path frictionless without making ordinary partner communication into fundraising?
  • Have guilt, shame, fear, artificial deadlines, special-access promises and promised returns been removed?

Evidence and reporting

  • Is every number supported by a record, a named counting method, or a stated source?
  • Are verified, reported, estimated and unverified claims clearly distinguished?
  • Have we avoided using numbers as proof that the ministry is spiritually successful?
  • Are testimonies represented accurately, without exaggeration or invented detail?
  • If information is unavailable, have we said so instead of filling the gap?

Scripture and quotation integrity

  • Is NKJV the primary translation, unless another version better serves the assigned purpose?
  • Has every intended direct quotation been checked against the exact named translation?
  • Is a fully quoted verse on its own line, with the version in the reference?
  • Does an inline quotation carry its reference inline?
  • Has King James wording such as GO YE been preserved where it was intentionally quoted that way?
  • Have paraphrases been used only when requested, and clearly distinguished from direct quotation?

Leadership, preaching and source integrity

  • Is the institutional voice distinct from Joel's and Nathan's personal ministry voices?
  • Is Joel's calling to the local Church, and Nathan's calling to take the local Church to the world, represented accurately where it matters?
  • If material came from preaching, were changes limited to punctuation unless style or content editing was specifically requested?
  • Have the founder's theology, recurring images and heart been preserved rather than replaced with generic professional language?

Channel and action

  • Does the execution fit its channel: web, social, email, preaching, partnership communication, or formal report?
  • For social media, does the post speak directly to the person unless it is intentionally informative?
  • Is there one primary action, rather than several competing requests?
  • Does the CTA belong to the initiative and to the stage of the relationship?
  • After a THE WORD communication, is partnership or the newsletter the normal continuing relationship, where appropriate?

The final red-flag pass

  • Did we make anything up?
  • Did we promise an outcome that God, the ministry, or the available evidence does not guarantee?
  • Did we use fear, shame, pressure, contempt or superiority to create action?
  • Did we make the ministry, the leader, the event, the app or the donor the hero instead of Jesus?
  • Did we weaken the Gospel, the Holy Spirit, the Great Commission or the local Church to gain acceptance?
  • Can the intended audience understand what this means, and what to do next?

Final decision. Pass only when every applicable doctrinal, integrity, Scripture, evidence and partnership item is satisfied. Escalate anything unresolved to the messaging owner before publication.
