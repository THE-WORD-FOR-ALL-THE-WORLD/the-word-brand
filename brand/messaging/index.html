01 · How To Use This GuideThe Word · Messaging Guide

One guide. Every voice.

This document is written for everyone who speaks or writes for THE WORD FOR ALL THE WORLD: leadership, team members, volunteers, and outside writers, designers, and agencies. It assumes no tribal knowledge. If you have never worked with us before, this guide is enough.

The Brand Guide (v4.4) governs how we look. This guide governs what we say. The two are companions: Law II (everything is a record), Law III (one house, named front doors), and Law IV (the institution signs its work) apply to words exactly as they apply to design.

The rule of rule: where wording in any older document, website, deck, or social post conflicts with this guide, this guide wins. Changes to this guide happen one way: proposed in writing, approved by Joel and Nathan, recorded in the changelog (Section 12 of the document below).

03The Record

How It Began

This is the canonical telling of the origin story. Tell it this way: in interviews, on the About page, from the stage. Bracketed fields are pending verification against the ministry record; per Law II, nothing unverified enters a founding document. Verify, unbracket, done.

In [2024], Joel Zimmer traveled to Uganda alongside [Inspiring Better Business]. As he stepped off the plane, a man named [David Kamanzi] met him with one question: "Will you help me train my people?" That question was the first seed of the School of the Local Church. 04Identity

What We Are, What We Are Not

The name THE WORD FOR ALL THE WORLD invites assumptions. Messaging corrects them plainly, without being defensive: one clear sentence and move on.

We are:

  • A Spirit-led ministry that strengthens local churches to do the Great Commission.
  • A servant of the local church: we come to serve their house, not build ours.
  • Evangelists and trainers: we preach, we stir, we equip, and we leave the church stronger than we found it.

We are not:

  • Not a Bible translation ministry. "THE WORD" is the Word preached, not the Word translated.
  • Not a denomination. We plant fire, not flags.
  • Not a replacement for the local church. If there is no local church to partner with in an area, we hold off. Our goal is never to create spiritual orphans.
05Filters

Values, Uniques & Guarantee

Four core values, three uniques, one guarantee. These are not slogans; they are filters. Content that violates a value gets killed no matter how good it looks.

  • Burn For Jesus. We burn with the passion of our relationship with Jesus and with His mission, the Great Commission.
  • Spirit-Led. Our spirit has the final say. We never violate it, and we always fear the Lord. Words are stewarded to build, edify, and impart life.
  • Selfless Love. Not concerned about credit, fame, or name recognition. Servant leadership: promoting and developing others first.
  • Full of Faith. We challenge giants with the Word of God and take the Kingdom by the force of faith.

Three uniques. The Holy Spirit is our PR man. We communicate frequently. Partnership is our strength.

The guarantee. The Holy Spirit runs our services.

"The Holy Spirit is our PR man" is internal and partner-facing language. In public copy, say it the way the guarantee says it: the Holy Spirit runs our services. There is no routine He can't interrupt.

07The Plan

The Journey: Clean · Burn · Train

The three priorities are a sequence, not a menu. A heart is cleaned, then it burns, then it is trained to keep burning. All journey messaging honors this order.

  • CLEAN · Revival To My City. Stirring the local church to return to their first love.
  • BURN · EVERY1 Movement. Empowering the local church to do the Great Commission and walk in God's calling.
  • TRAIN · School of the Local Church. Training the local church to know their authority in Christ and build a real relationship with Jesus.

How one weekend tells the whole story. We arrive in a city Thursday or Friday. Evenings are Revival To My City services: worship, preaching, altar calls, healing, testimonies captured. Hearts get cleaned out by the power of God. Saturday morning is the EVERY1 interest meeting: what the movement is, how to join, and the first outreach. Participants personally evangelize in the community that same morning. From there, they sign up for the School of the Local Church, starting with the free personal evangelism course. One person can be in all three at once, and often is.

Each initiative carries a full messaging document and a brand guide of its own, on official letterhead, in the Documents and Letterhead sections of this portal.

08Audiences

The Five People We Speak To

Every piece of content is aimed at one of these five people. Know which one before you write a word. The "what they need to hear" line is the heart of the message: say it in your own words, but say that.

The Local Pastor US & International

They want: to multiply disciples and lead revival without burning out.

Their pain: exhaustion, plateaued growth, no discipleship pathway.

"We come to serve your church, not build ours. When we leave, your people stay, on fire and equipped."

First step: host a Revival To My City · train leaders through the School.

The Hungry Church Member The Pew, Awake or Asleep

They want: to grow spiritually and live on mission.

Their pain: dry faith. The conviction that "there must be more."

"There is more, and it isn't a program. It's a real relationship with Jesus, and it starts with turning back to your first love."

First step: join the EVERY1 Movement · start the School.

The Lost Online Searcher 18 to 30 · Digital-First

They want: truth, healing, identity, purpose.

Their pain: anxiety, loneliness, distrust of religion, hungry for what's real.

"What you're looking for has a name. You can have a real relationship with Jesus, starting today."

First step: watch a testimony → request prayer → begin the intro course.

The Legacy Leader 66+ · Finishing Well

They want: to pass the torch and leave their people stronger.

Their pain: feeling forgotten; fear the work dies with them.

"Your fire isn't finished. The next generation needs what you carry."

First step: mentor through the School · cover the movement in prayer · give.

The Kingdom Partner Businesses & Ministries

They want: eternal impact with their influence and resources.

Their pain: unsure whom to trust; tired of shallow ministry.

"Fund what heaven is doing. Every number we report comes from the official record. You will always know exactly what your partnership did."

First step: sponsor a Revival To My City · scholarship School students.

A note on Gen Z (18 to 25): they trust testimony over performance and vulnerability over polish. Lead with real stories of transformation, never with production value. They can smell hype, which is fine, because we don't use it.
09Voice

Voice & Vocabulary

Bold, clear, simple, direct. Pastoral with prophetic urgency. Invitation-first. Short, punchy sentences. Never polished-corporate. Never hype. Never churchy. If a new believer couldn't understand it, rewrite it.

The filter: Would Nathan actually say this? Does it point people to Jesus Himself? Is it clear to a new believer? Does it invite response? If unsure, simplify and center Jesus.

Words we carry. This recurring language is our fingerprint. Use these phrases exactly. They are load-bearing.

PhraseWhat it carries
Real relationship with JesusThe gospel in five words. The good news is not religion. It's Him.
First loveRevelation 3. The heart of every RTMC message: return.
Fire starts fireHow the mission spreads. We burn; the dry catch.
Revival is here, not comingOur posture. We announce a fact, we don't market a hope.

The Holy Spirit runs our servicesThe guarantee. There is no routine He can't interrupt.

Words we never use. Three ban categories, with starter lists. These lists are proposals for the v1.0 review: strike or keep each one. Theological words (repentance, salvation, Holy Spirit baptism) are never banned; they are explained. The test is always the filter: clear to a new believer.

Hype
insane · epic · unreal · next-level · game-changer · "God showed up and showed out" · "you won't believe" · walls of 🔥 emojis · ALL-CAPS excitement
Hype manufactures a feeling. We report what God actually did. The record is more powerful than the adjective.
Corporate Polish
leverage · synergy · solutions · optimize · engage stakeholders · "excited to announce" · comprehensive · robust
We are a ministry, not a vendor. Corporate language makes the guide the hero and the mission a product.
Churchy Insider Talk
traveling mercies · hedge of protection · "love on" · "do life together" · "press in" · "contend for breakthrough" · "a God thing" · "fresh anointing" (unexplained) · "blessed to announce"
Insider language tells a new believer this isn't for them. Everything we say is for them.

Rewrites: before and after.

Never thisAlways this
"🔥🔥 INSANE night!! God showed UP and showed OUT!!""Friday night in [city]: [n] people heard the gospel. [n] turned their hearts back to Jesus. The fire is spreading." (numbers from the official record only)
"Join us as we press into a fresh anointing and contend for breakthrough.""Come. Jesus is moving. Bring the person you've been praying for."
"We provide comprehensive discipleship solutions for local church partners.""We train the local church to walk with Jesus."

Standing rules. Short sentences. One idea each. Say Jesus by name, not "faith," not "spirituality." Never invent a statistic, a quote, or a testimony; the record or nothing (Law II). Never manufacture a catchphrase: if a line has to be explained to be catchy, kill it. Invitation before information: every piece ends with something the reader can do.

10Policy

Proof & Testimony Policy

This guide contains no statistics on purpose. Numbers live in one place, the official ministry record, and messaging cites that record rather than freezing figures that will age. Per the Minister Agreement: official numbers only, never estimates, never memory. A guide that hardcodes stats violates Law II the day the record updates; a guide that points to the record never does.

Testimonies: the four slots. Every testimony is a record of what God did: real, consented, and named. Never fabricated, never composited, never anonymous.

Before
Life as it was, in the person's own words.
Encounter
The moment Jesus intervened: where and when.
Transformation
What changed: specific, verifiable, honest.
Outcome
Life now, and the invitation to respond.
