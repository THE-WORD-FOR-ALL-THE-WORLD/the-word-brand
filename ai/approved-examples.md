# Approved Examples

> Canonical source: <https://brand.theword.world/ai/manifest.json>

Worked output that passes the audit. Pattern-match against these when you are unsure what "on
brand" looks like in practice. Every example is drawn from published material or built from the
guides' own rendered examples.

Bracketed fields (`[n]`, `[city]`) are deliberate. They mark values that come from the official
ministry record and must be filled from it, never guessed.

## Homepage hero (parent institution)

Laws I, IV, and V. Serif headline in sentence case with one italic word, mission line beneath,
one primary and one ghost action, all over documentary footage on the Midnight scrim.

> **EVERY1 WILL KNOW THE NAME JESUS** *(eyebrow, DM Sans 600, letterspaced)*
>
> # Every generation seeks a revival. No one told them it is *already here.*
>
> Strengthening the local church to fulfill the Great Commission, until EVERY1 knows the name
> Jesus.
>
> **[ Partner With Us ]**  [ See What's Happening ]

Why it passes: one idea, sentence case, a single italic word, the exact public mission line,
invitation before information, white type on the scrim.

## Priority panel (initiative)

Law I. One bold word per panel, mission line beneath, each panel linking to its own named page.

> *(full-bleed field footage, Midnight scrim)*
>
> ## CLEAN
> Stirring the local church to return to their first love.
>
> A ministry of THE WORD FOR ALL THE WORLD

Three panels, never four or five variations. CLEAN, BURN, TRAIN, always in that order.

## Testimony

The four slots, in order, with a real and consented attribution.

> **BEFORE** [Life as it was, in the person's own words.]
>
> **ENCOUNTER** [The moment Jesus intervened, where and when.]
>
> **TRANSFORMATION** [What changed. Specific, verifiable, honest.]
>
> **OUTCOME** [Life now.] Jesus is still doing this. Come and see.
>
> **[Name] · [City] · Shared with permission**

Why it passes: the structure is filled from a real story or left bracketed, it closes with an
invitation, and it is named.

## The three summaries

Use these verbatim when an introduction, bio, or boilerplate is needed.

**One sentence.**
THE WORD FOR ALL THE WORLD strengthens the local church to fulfill the Great Commission, until
EVERY1 knows the name Jesus.

**One paragraph.**
THE WORD FOR ALL THE WORLD is a Spirit-led ministry that strengthens the local church to fulfill
the Great Commission. Through Revival To My City, the EVERY1 Movement, and the School of the Local
Church, we stir believers back to their first love, send them to the lost, and train them to walk
with Jesus, until EVERY1 knows the name Jesus.

**The full telling.**
THE WORD FOR ALL THE WORLD began with a prophecy and a question on a Ugandan tarmac: "Will you
help me train my people?" We are a Spirit-led ministry that strengthens the local church to fulfill
the Great Commission. We don't replace the local church. We serve it. Through Revival To My City we
stir congregations back to their first love. Through the EVERY1 Movement we send EVERY1 in the
church to EVERY1 outside of it. Through the School of the Local Church we train believers to know
their authority in Christ and build a real relationship with Jesus. Fire starts fire, until EVERY1
knows the name Jesus.

## Correcting an assumption about the name

One clear sentence, then move on. Never defensive.

> THE WORD FOR ALL THE WORLD is not a Bible translation ministry. "THE WORD" is the Word preached,
> not the Word translated. We are evangelists and trainers who strengthen the local church.
