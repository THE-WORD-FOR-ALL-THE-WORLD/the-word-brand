# Drop zone and provenance, not published

Working files land here on their way to becoming masters. Nothing in this folder is
published: the build ignores any directory starting with an underscore, so nothing here
reaches `/ai/assets.json`, the sitemap, or the site.

To add or replace a mark, put the best available source here and say what it is:

1. Original design file: `.ai`, `.svg`, `.pdf`, or a Figma or Canva link
2. Highest-resolution raster you have

A file leaves this folder only as an approved vector master under `_masters/`, recorded in
`ai-source/asset-notes.json`, with a changelog entry naming who approved it.

## What is in here now

The five THE WORD wordmark files exactly as supplied on 19 August 2026, before any
processing. They are kept as the record of what was approved. The masters in `_masters/`
are these same files with the background square removed and the ink moved onto the root
element, which is all that stands between what was handed over and what the site publishes.

The five EVERY1 files supplied on 19 August 2026: `every1-wordmark-navy`,
`every1-wordmark-inverse`, `every1-vision-navy`, `every1-vision-inverse`, and
`every1-favicon`. These were supplied with EVERY set as live text on a fallback system
font stack, and with the 1 drawn to three different proportions across the five files, so
they could not be published as handed over: a mark is artwork, not live text, and one
movement has one numeral. What is published at `assets/logos/every1/` is the same design
with EVERY set in DM Sans 900 and converted to outlines per the family grammar in Brand
Guide §11, and with a single numeral, normalised from the favicon drawing, reused at every
size. Background squares are dropped from every form except the app icon, where the
Midnight plate is the artwork.
