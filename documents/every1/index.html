The EVERY1 Movement

Initiative Messaging Document · August 2026

The foundation. In December 2019 this ministry received the prophecy that stands as its founding document. The word came before the work. THE WORD FOR ALL THE WORLD exists to strengthen the local church to fulfill the Great Commission, until EVERY1 knows the name Jesus. The ministry orders its work as one journey in three parts, CLEAN, BURN, and TRAIN, held always in that order: a heart is cleaned, then it burns, then it is trained to keep burning.

The Great Commission will not be fulfilled by pulpits alone. Every believer is the minister, sent by God everywhere they go.

This document records what the initiative is, in the words of the ministry

Section 1.Name and Standing

The initiative is named the EVERY1 Movement, always styled EVERY1. It is a named front door of THE WORD FOR ALL THE WORLD. It is not a separate organization, not a separate home, and not a denomination. It carries no endorsement line and no parent lockup: this door is the recorded exception to Law III, and it stands on its own wherever its name appears.

Section 2.Place in the Journey

The EVERY1 Movement is the second movement of the journey. Its word is BURN. A cleaned heart catches fire, and fire starts fire.

Section 3.Purpose

To empower the local church to do the Great Commission and walk in God's calling. In one line: EVERY1 in the church going to EVERY1 outside of the church.

In plain words: The EVERY1 Movement sends the church out.
Section 4.What It Is

A movement of ordinary believers who share Jesus where they already live, work, and study. You are the minister, sent by God everywhere you go.

It is a lifestyle, not an event. Membership is simple: you have shared Jesus with at least one person recently. That is the movement.

Section 5.Who May Join

Every believer. There is no maturity requirement. When you are born again, you qualify.

The movement carries special fire for the young and the newly saved: their fire kindles faster and hotter.

Section 6.The First Three Steps
  • Join the weekly prayer meeting, praying for the lost.
  • Share Jesus with one person this week.
  • Take the free personal evangelism course, offered through the School of the Local Church.
Section 7.The Words It Carries
EVERY1 in the church for EVERY1 outside the churchYou are the ministerFire starts fireA lifestyle, not an event
Section 8.On the Record

EVERY1 is the one door that stands on its own. On the YouVersion model, it carries no endorsement line and no parent lockup, across the movement, the app, and the activation platform alike. That exception stands on record in the Brand Guide. Everything else in the Brand Messaging Guide applies to it in full.

Section 9.Authority and Amendment

This document is part of the Brand Messaging Guide and carries its authority. It changes one way: a proposed edit in writing, approval by Joel Zimmer and Nathan Zimmer, and a recorded entry in the changelog. No silent edits, ever.
