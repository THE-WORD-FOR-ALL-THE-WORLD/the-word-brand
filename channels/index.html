The Channel Layer

What this looks like where you are building it.

The brand does not change per surface. The sizes, the limits, and the file formats do.

A canvas size, a character limit, and a safe area are facts about a platform rather than facts about the brand, which is why no visual guide states them and why they are recorded here instead. The rules under each surface are the brand's, and every entry names the audit checks that apply to it.

The machine-readable copies are /ai/channels.json and /ai/copy-bank.json. Anyone assembling a campaign should work from those rather than from this page.

web

Websites and landing pages

Anything rendered in a browser that is not an application: the portal, a campaign page, a partner microsite.

Specifications

measure
660px maximum for extended reading, roughly 66 characters
breakpoints
600px, 720px, 1080px
socialCard
1200x630 PNG, required on every page
favicon
32px and 16px PNG, plus a 180px apple-touch-icon

Setup

  • Link @site/assets/brand.css and @site/assets/fonts/fonts.css. That is the whole setup.
  • Copy markup from @site/components rather than rebuilding a component from its description.

Rules

  • Three to five priorities on a page. A sixth is a second page.
  • Every priority word links somewhere real. No decorative priorities.
  • A wide table scrolls inside its own frame. The page body never scrolls sideways.
  • The focus ring is never removed.

app

Applications

The initiative applications, and any dashboard or logged-in surface.

Specifications

touchTarget
44px minimum on every control, whatever its drawn height
appIcon
1024x1024 source, the glyph on Midnight, no text
splash
Midnight ground, the reversed horizontal lockup, nothing else
states
every control needs default, hover, focus, active, disabled, loading, and empty

Setup

  • Install @theword/brand and @theword/ui. Import the stylesheet once at the root.
  • Load the three faces with next/font/google, which self-hosts them at build time.
  • Keep theming in CSS custom properties, not a JavaScript theme object, so it works unchanged in server components.

Rules

  • Error and warning states use the recorded functional colours. Never Flame, never Ember: Ember means the action, and an error is not an action.
  • An error names what is wrong and how to fix it. Colour never carries the message alone.
  • A dark surface is Midnight, not a darkened Parchment.
  • Loading is a wash placeholder, never a spinner over a blank screen.

email

Email and newsletters

Every newsletter, announcement, and transactional message.

Specifications

width
600px
logo
PNG at 400px or 800px. Never SVG: most clients strip it.
subjectLine
50 characters or fewer, so it survives a phone
preheader
90 characters or fewer, and never a repeat of the subject
images
PNG only, with alt text that carries the meaning when images are blocked

Setup

  • Build from the email-layout and email-button components. Tables and inline styles, not divs and a stylesheet.
  • Georgia stands in for the serifs and Arial for DM Sans. The webfont will not load, so the fallback is the design.

Rules

  • Every colour is stated explicitly on every cell. A dark-mode client inverts anything left to a default.
  • One call to action per email.
  • Every email carries a preheader, a line saying why the person is receiving it, and a working unsubscribe.
  • The destination URL appears as text in the footer, for clients that strip links.

Search ads

Google and Microsoft responsive search ads.

Specifications

headline
30 characters, 15 per ad group
description
90 characters, 4 per ad group
displayPath
15 characters per path segment, two segments
sitelinkText
25 characters
sitelinkDescription
35 characters per line, two lines
callout
25 characters

Setup

  • Assemble every headline and description from the copy bank. Nothing is written at the keyboard in the ad platform.
  • A new string joins the bank by passing the audit once, and is then reusable for good.

Rules

  • Never a statistic, a claim of outcome, or a number that is not in the official ministry record.
  • Never a banned word. The hype list exists because ad copy is where it creeps back in.
  • Sentence case. Never all caps, never an exclamation mark.
  • The believer and the local church are the hero, not the ministry.

Display and Performance Max

Google Display, Performance Max asset groups, and any placement that auto-crops.

Specifications

landscape
1200x628, ratio 1.91:1
square
1200x1200, ratio 1:1
portrait
960x1200, ratio 4:5
logoSquare
1200x1200, the glyph on Midnight
logoLandscape
1200x300, the horizontal lockup on Midnight
safeArea
keep type and the mark within the centre 80%: the platform crops the edges without asking

Rules

video

Video

Conference recordings, field reports, reels, and every loop on a page.

Specifications

loop
6 to 15 seconds, muted, looping, with a still poster frame
titleSafe
keep type within the centre 90% for a 16:9 export
lowerThird
sits on the scrim bar, never straight on the footage
subtitles
DM Sans 500 or heavier, on the scrim bar, on every spoken word
export
H.264 MP4, 1080p at minimum, plus a poster JPG at the same frame

Rules

  • Every loop ships with the still that stands in for it under a reduced-motion preference.
  • Nothing animated bounces or springs.
  • Every subject is a co-labourer in the gospel, never an object of pity.
  • Consent is obtained before capture, not asked for afterwards.

print

Print

Flyers, banners, programmes, certificates, vehicles, and anything a press or a vendor produces.

Specifications

colourSpace
CMYK. The recorded values are an unprofiled starting point and are confirmed on a proof.
bleed
3mm on every trimmed edge
safeMargin
5mm inside the trim, and never less than the mark's own clear space
resolution
300dpi for any raster placed in a print document
minimumMarkWidth
40mm for the horizontal lockup, 25mm for the stacked

Setup

  • Send the vector and the recorded colour values. Never send a PNG to a printer.
  • Import the palette from the Adobe swatch file rather than typing hex values into a document.

Rules

  • A vendor never samples a colour off a screenshot.
  • Embroidery and engraving take the one-colour black file, which has no anti-aliasing to misread.
  • A corrected colour from a proof comes back into the guide through a changelog entry, never as a note in someone's inbox.
